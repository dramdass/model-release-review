# Draft participation terms

- Anyone can join. We welcome affected users, model builders, maintainers, and reviewers from different regions and languages.
- Open a GitHub issue to join, propose a case, or raise a scoring concern. For a case, include the outcome, how to reproduce it, and what passes.
- Share only material you have permission to publish. Keep private information out of public issues.
- Disclose relevant conflicts. A second person reviews each case. The open review groups decide which cases enter and publish the reason.
- Discuss asynchronously and at regular open meetings with rotating times. Record what changed, what remains disputed, and who will follow up.

[Read the four principles](README.md) or [join the discussion](https://github.com/dramdass/model-release-review/issues/new).
